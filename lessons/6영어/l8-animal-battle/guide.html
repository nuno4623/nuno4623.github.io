1시작하기
어떤 동물을 비교할지 소개해요. Let's compare them!으로 시작하면 좋아요.

ANIMAL BATTLE!

Look at these animals. Giraffes and elephants live in Africa. Let's compare them!
2특징 소개하기
This is ~.
It has ~ and ~.
and로 특징 두 개를 한 문장에 묶어요.
eats, can으로 그 동물에 대한 사실을 더해요.
Giraffe
This is a giraffe. It has a long neck and long legs. It eats leaves from tall trees.
The giraffe is taller than the elephant.
The giraffe is faster than the elephant.
VS
Elephant
This is an elephant. It has big ears and a long nose. It can carry heavy things.
The elephant is bigger than the giraffe.
The elephant is heavier than the giraffe.
The elephant is stronger than the giraffe.
3비교하기
형용사 + er + than
tall → taller
fast → faster
철자 주의!
big → bigger
heavy → heavier
Who is the winner? The giraffe is fast, but the elephant is very strong.
I think the elephant wins because it is stronger than the giraffe.
4내 생각 쓰기
but으로 다른 점을, because로 이유를 써요.
